Common questions

Is it really free for couples?

Yes. The full dashboard — budget, guest list, seating, checklist, and vendor messaging — is free. Plus is an optional upgrade, not a requirement.

How are vendors vetted?

Every vendor submits references, licensing where applicable, and a portfolio review before they're approved to list.

Can vendors cancel anytime?

Yes, vendor subscriptions are month to month with no lock-in contract.

Do you take a commission on bookings?

No. Vendors pay a flat monthly listing fee — never a percentage of what they earn from your wedding.
